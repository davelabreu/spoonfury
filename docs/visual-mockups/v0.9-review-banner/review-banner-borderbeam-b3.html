B3 Soft Glow — Iterations

Five mutations of the soft glow beam. Each shown with indigo (active review) and amber (warming up) states side by side.

B3a / Original (baseline)

The B3 you picked. 6s, wide tail, blur 0.5px, opacity 0.8. Shown for reference.

B3b / Tighter & Brighter

Shorter tail, sharper leading edge, less blur. More precision, less dream. 5s loop.

B3c / Comet Tail

Very long fade-in tail covering almost half the perimeter. Dreamy, slow aurora feel. 8s loop, heavier blur.

B3d / Double Glow

The original B3 beam plus a wider, blurry outer halo that follows it. Feels premium, like a neon sign in fog. 6s loop.

B3e / Glacial

Very slow (10s), half opacity. You barely notice it's moving — then you do. Subtle prestige.
