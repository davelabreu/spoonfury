# Future Shopping List Enhancements

## Ingredient Scaling by Weight / Minimum Purchase Qty
Recipes specify ideal quantities (e.g., 2 eggs), but stores sell in minimum units (a dozen eggs, 500g butter). Spoonfury should help users:
- Understand minimum purchase quantities for ingredients
- Stretch surplus ingredients across multiple meals/recipes in their list
- Scale ingredient quantities when cooking multiple servings

## Total Shopping Cost
Show estimated total cost for the entire shopping list. Requires price data per ingredient (manual entry, or API integration with grocery services).

## Recipe Images
Add recipe images to recipe cards throughout the app. In the shopping list, recipe cards would show a thumbnail alongside the title and quantity controls.
